The problem

The floor is short. The QR code does not serve.

On a busy night the captain cannot stand at every table. A QR menu can list the dishes. It does not greet anyone, it does not help a mixed table choose, and it does not keep a round off the wok until a person has looked at it.

Not enough captains

Heritage houses run on people who remember regulars, portions, and how a family likes the pot. Those people are hard to hire, and they cannot be at twenty tables at once.

A list is not a host

Guests still wave for help, still over-order, still miss the rice. The kitchen receives whatever was tapped, with no one on the floor to say yes first.

How it works

Greet, plan, then ask the floor.

Each step is something your captain already does. TableHua does it at every table, and still waits for your staff.

1

Greet

Ah Hua welcomes the table and asks how many people are eating.

2

Plan

Guests set a budget, a spice level, and what to leave out. They can edit the suggestion before anything is sent.

3

Approve the round

The table sends a round. Staff check it. The kitchen does not start until that yes.

4

Kitchen

Approved tickets show on the kitchen screen. A declined or waiting round never reaches the pass.

5

PayNow split

The bill can be split for the table, with a sample PayNow code. TableHua does not take the payment.

6

Membership

Regulars can keep a stored-value balance and come back to a host that remembers the house, not a new QR each visit.

Languages

FAQ

Before you write to us

Does it work alongside the POS we already use?

Yes. TableHua sits with the till you already have. Staff approve each round before it is cooked. It does not replace your POS.

Do we need new hardware?

No. Diners use their own phones on the table link. Staff and the kitchen use a browser on a phone, tablet, or computer you already have.

Do you deliver, run the till, or take the payment?

No. Delivery, A full point-of-sale, Taking the payment ourselves. PayNow on the bill is a split your guests can pay to you. The concept demo charges S$0.00.
